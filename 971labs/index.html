Process

How a project runs

Four stages. You see working software every two weeks, and you can stop after any stage.

  1. 01 · 2 weeks

    Discover

    We interview your team and users, map the systems involved, and agree scope, timeline and a fixed price.

  2. 02 · 2–4 weeks

    Design

    Clickable prototypes in Arabic and English, tested with real users and approved by you before development starts.

  3. 03 · Two-week sprints

    Build

    Small releases to a staging environment you can use, with code review, automated tests and a demo at the end of every sprint.

  4. 04 · Ongoing

    Launch and run

    We launch, monitor and support what we built, with agreed response times, or hand everything over to your team.
